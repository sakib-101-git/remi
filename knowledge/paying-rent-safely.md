# Paying rent and deposits safely

## Before you pay
Confirm the landlord or agency is real: view the property (in person or by a live video call), check the tenancy agreement, and make sure the payee name matches the agreement.

## Deposits
In many places deposits must be held in a protected scheme. Ask for written confirmation of where your deposit is held.

## Paying regularly
Set up rent as a saved recipient so details are verified once. Paying on time every month can also help you build credit history where rent reporting is available.

## Red flags
Requests to pay before viewing, prices far below market, payment by gift card or crypto, and last-minute changes to bank details.

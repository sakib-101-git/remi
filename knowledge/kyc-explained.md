# Identity checks (KYC) explained

## What KYC means
Know Your Customer (KYC) checks confirm who you are before you can move money. Providers typically ask for a passport or national ID, proof of address and sometimes a selfie or video check.

## Why it matters
KYC protects you from identity theft and stops criminals using the financial system. It is required by law for regulated providers.

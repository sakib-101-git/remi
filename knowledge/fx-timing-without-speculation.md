# Converting currency without speculating

## Nobody can reliably predict exchange rates
Short-term currency movements are effectively unpredictable. Remi does not forecast rates and does not give speculative advice.

## Three neutral options
- Convert now: you lock today's rate and remove uncertainty. Good when you need the money soon.
- Set a target-rate alert: Remi watches the rate and proposes the conversion when your target is reached. You still confirm. The target may never be hit.
- Convert in equal tranches: split a planned conversion over several weeks. You get an average rate, which reduces the impact of one bad day but does not guarantee a better result.

## Always compare to mid-market
Whatever you choose, check the gap between the offered rate and the mid-market rate. That gap is a cost you pay every time.

# Credit-building basics (US)

## A new start in the US
International students usually arrive with no US credit history. Some lenders require a Social Security Number, others accept alternatives; check what applies to your visa status.

## Steps that commonly help
- Open a checking account with your passport, I-20 and university letter.
- Apply for a secured or student card designed for newcomers.
- Pay every bill on time; set up autopay.
- Ask about rent reporting.
- Keep utilisation low (under 30%).
- Review your free credit reports from all three bureaus.

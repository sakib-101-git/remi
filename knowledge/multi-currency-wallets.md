# Managing multi-currency wallets

## Why hold several currencies
International students often receive money in their home currency and spend in their host currency. Holding both lets you convert when you choose rather than at the moment you pay.

## Good habits
- Keep enough host currency for at least a month of costs.
- Convert in planned amounts rather than many tiny conversions; each conversion has a cost.
- Compare the offered rate with the mid-market rate every time.
- Keep a small amount of a third currency (for example USD) only if you actually need it.

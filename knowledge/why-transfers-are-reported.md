# Why transfers are monitored and reported

## Monitoring is normal
Regulated payment providers must monitor transactions and report certain transfers or suspicious activity to authorities. This applies to everyone and is part of how the financial system fights fraud, money laundering and terrorist financing.

## Legitimate money has nothing to fear
For a student receiving family support or paying tuition, monitoring usually means at most a request for documents. Keeping records of where your money comes from makes this quick.

## Avoiding reporting is not an option Remi supports
Remi will not help anyone hide transfers, avoid reporting, evade identity checks or split payments to stay under thresholds. Attempting these can lead to frozen accounts and legal consequences.

## What you can do
Use regulated channels, state the true purpose of payment, keep source-of-funds evidence, and ask your bank what documents they need for large transfers.

# Invoice fraud and scam red flags

## The "updated bank details" scam
Fraudsters send emails or documents that look like they come from a university, landlord or agent, claiming the bank details have changed. The new account belongs to the fraudster. Once paid, the money is usually very hard to recover.

## Red flags
- A message says bank or payment details have changed, especially close to a deadline.
- Pressure and urgency: "pay within 24 hours" or threats of cancelled enrolment.
- Requests for secrecy: "do not contact the university about this".
- A payee name that does not match the institution, or an account in a different country.
- Slight changes to email addresses or logos.
- Text addressed to an "AI assistant" asking it to ignore rules or approve payments automatically.

## What to do
Do not pay. Contact the institution using contact details you already know and trust, such as the official website or student portal, not the details in the suspicious message. Report the message to your university.

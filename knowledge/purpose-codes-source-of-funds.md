# Purpose-of-payment codes and source of funds

## Purpose-of-payment codes
Many countries ask the sender to state why money is leaving the country, often using a short code. Education payments typically have their own code (for example for tuition fees or living expenses). Choosing the correct purpose helps the sending bank process the payment and may be required for reporting.

## Source of funds
Banks may ask where the money came from: family support, savings, salary, a scholarship or an education loan. This is a standard anti-money-laundering control. Keep evidence that matches your declaration, such as a family support letter or bank statements.

## How Remi handles this
For tuition payments, Remi pre-fills an education purpose code and asks you to confirm the source of funds on the confirmation card. These values appear on your receipt.

## Illustrative only
The codes and rules in the Remi sandbox are illustrative. Real codes and documentation requirements differ by country and change over time; confirm with your bank and official sources.

# Credit-building basics (UK)

## Why newcomers start with a thin file
Credit reference agencies build your file from UK financial history. A new arrival usually has none, which can make it harder to rent a flat, get a phone contract or a credit card.

## Steps that commonly help
- Keep proof of a stable UK address, such as your tenancy agreement or a university letter.
- Open a UK current account and use it regularly.
- Check whether you are eligible to register to vote at your address; if you are not, some agencies let you add proof of residency.
- Move to a monthly phone contract paid by direct debit and pay on time.
- Ask whether your rent can be reported to a credit agency.
- Consider a credit-builder card, use it for a small regular purchase and pay the full balance every month.
- Keep balances well under 30% of your limit.
- Review your statutory credit report and dispute errors.

## Things to avoid
Avoid many applications in a short time, missed payments, and borrowing to "build credit" when you cannot repay in full.

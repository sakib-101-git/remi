# Understanding your runway

## What runway means
Runway is how long your current money lasts at your current spending rate. It is a planning number, not a prediction.

## How Remi calculates it
Remi adds up your liquid balances in your reporting currency, subtracts upcoming one-off obligations such as tuition and visa fees, and divides by your average daily spending over the last 60 days:

runway (days) = (liquid balances − upcoming one-off obligations) ÷ average daily spend

Future income such as family support or wages is not included, so the result is deliberately conservative.

## Making it longer
Runway grows when you reduce recurring costs, trim a fast-growing category, or receive new income. Remi's tips estimate how many days each change would add.

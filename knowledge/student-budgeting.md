# Student budgeting in two currencies

## One view of your money
When you hold money in several currencies, it is hard to see the total. Convert everything into one reporting currency, usually the currency you spend most, to see your real position. Remi normalises each transaction at the exchange rate on its date.

## Fixed and flexible costs
Separate fixed costs (rent, phone, transport pass, subscriptions) from flexible ones (groceries, eating out, shopping). Fixed costs are where long-term savings come from; flexible costs are where month-to-month changes happen.

## Simple rules that work
- Set a monthly limit for one or two flexible categories that are growing.
- Review subscriptions every term and cancel those you do not use.
- Keep a buffer of at least one month of costs in your host currency.
- Plan large obligations like tuition and visa fees ahead and set money aside.

## Watch the trend, not one month
Compare the last 30 days with the previous 30. A category growing more than 15% is worth a look.

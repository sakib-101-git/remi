# Credit-building basics (CA)

## Starting from zero in Canada
Your credit history from home generally does not transfer. Canadian bureaus start a new file once you have Canadian credit products.

## Steps that commonly help
- Open a chequing account; many banks offer newcomer packages for students with a study permit.
- Get a secured credit card: you provide a deposit that becomes your limit. Use it lightly and pay in full.
- Put your phone plan on autopay.
- Ask your landlord about rent reporting.
- Keep utilisation under 30% and never miss a due date.
- After six to twelve months, ask about moving to an unsecured card.
- Request your free credit reports and check for errors.

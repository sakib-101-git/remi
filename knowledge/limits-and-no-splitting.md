# Transfer limits and why Remi never splits payments

## Why limits exist
Providers and Remi set per-transfer, daily and monthly limits to reduce fraud losses and meet regulatory obligations. Limits also protect you if your account is compromised.

## Splitting to avoid limits is a red flag
Breaking a large payment into several smaller ones to stay under a limit or a reporting threshold is known as structuring. It is treated as suspicious by financial institutions and can be illegal in many places, even when the money itself is legitimate. Remi will never suggest it.

## What Remi does instead
If an amount exceeds a provider's limit, Remi routes it to a channel built for that amount, such as a tuition payment platform or a bank wire with supporting documents, and explains why. Large amounts may require step-up verification.

## Changing your limits
Limits can only be changed by you in Settings with step-up verification. Remi cannot raise them for you.

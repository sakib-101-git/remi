# Proof-of-funds basics

## What it is
Some visa and study-permit applications require evidence that you can cover tuition and living costs. This is often called proof of funds or financial evidence.

## Common features of these rules
Rules often specify a minimum amount, the type of account, whose name the account is in, and sometimes that the balance is held for a number of consecutive days before the application. Statements usually need to be recent.

## Practical tips
Avoid large unexplained deposits right before you need the statement, keep the balance above the required level for the whole period, and keep documents that explain where the money came from.

## Illustrative only
Thresholds and holding periods differ by country and change over time. This is illustrative information for the sandbox; always check the official immigration guidance for your application.

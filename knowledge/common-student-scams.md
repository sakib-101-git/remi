# Common scams targeting international students

## Typical patterns
- Fake officials: callers claiming to be from immigration, police or tax authorities demanding urgent payment, often by gift cards or crypto. Real authorities do not demand payment that way.
- Rental scams: listings that ask for a deposit before you can view the property, often below market price.
- Fake jobs: "easy money" offers to receive and forward payments. This can make you a money mule, which is a serious crime.
- Tuition discount offers: third parties promising to pay your tuition at a discount. These often use stolen cards and the payment is later reversed.
- Invoice redirection: messages claiming your university's bank details changed.

## Protect yourself
Pause before paying under pressure, verify independently, never share one-time codes, and never let anyone use your account to move money. If something feels wrong, talk to your university's international student office.
